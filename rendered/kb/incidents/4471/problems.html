The three problems

parser: day and month swap. The shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so 7 April becomes 4 July. This is what #4471 says it is, and the queued fix is for this one.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. At day-first tenants people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it day first. The date swaps in the same way, so it looks like the parser. It is not: these reports come only through the form, only at tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch them; the fix is the hint.

one-day: the date moves by one day. A few reports say the day moved, not the month: “Tuesday 12 May” on the invite, “Monday 11 May” in the calendar; a client in Lisbon got Tuesday and a client in Toronto got Monday. Day and month did not swap. The desk read these as a one-day move and filed them under the parser by tenant. They are a different bug, and the parser fix leaves them where they are.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Merging on subject (decision 0001) put every “wrong date” report in one place. Subjects say how a report feels, not what broke.
  • Intake tags do not tell the problems apart. Intake tags a report hint when the reporter’s browser runs a US locale; the problem depends on the tenant and the way the report came in.
  • The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice. Count by id.

For the desk

  • Count a big ticket by what its reports show, then split it.
  • Keep #4471 for the parser. Move the hint and one-day reports to their own tickets.
